Email templates (backup)

Four angles on the same program, so Al can pick whichever fits how well he knows the person he's writing to. Each one copies with formatting and images intact, ready to paste straight into Gmail.

How to use these: click "Copy email" on whichever angle fits, then paste (Cmd/Ctrl + V) directly into a new Gmail draft, formatting, images, and links come along. Swap in the real name, tweak a line or two to sound like you, and send. A few notes on picking an angle:
  • Rice Lilie Lab angle, best for Rice-affiliated contacts who'd want to hear the institutional connection first.
  • Jackie Fisher angle, best for people who know Jackie or care about a founder's story more than credentials.
  • EO Houston angle, best for someone who'd be motivated by the community and network they'd be joining, not just the coursework.
  • Al Danto angle, written in your own voice, for people who know you personally and would respond to a direct note from you.
Mixing paragraphs from two angles into one email is fine, these are starting points, not scripts. Reference or attach the one-page flier below with any of these.

Al Danto angle

Personal note

Written in Al's own voice, for contacts who know him directly.

SubjectA Houston accelerator I helped build, thought of you

Hi [Name],

I wanted to share something I've been part of building: EO Houston Momentum, a two-year accelerator for Houston business owners ready to break past $1 million in revenue, run by Entrepreneurs' Organization Houston, a nonprofit that's part of a global network of 20,000+ entrepreneurs in 224 chapters.

After exiting my own company, I refined the curriculum with input from Jackie Fisher, a Rice alum and founder herself. It's built around real experience shared by entrepreneurs who've actually scaled past $1 million, with direct access to EO Houston's network of 200+ businesses who've done it. It's not a Rice program and it doesn't require a Rice degree to join. It's built for real operators, with 8 months of classes a year, a confidential peer Forum that meets monthly year-round, and a community of founders who actually show up for each other.

Momentum is for owners or co-founders with:

  • Yearly revenue over $250,000
  • A drive to build past $1 million in under two years

Applications for the cohort beginning October 2026 close September 15, 2026.

If this sounds like you, or you know a Houston founder who's ready, take a look: eomomentum.com, or learn about the chapter at eohouston.com

Happy to answer questions directly.

Al Danto
Program Director, EO Houston Momentum
Senior Lecturer in Entrepreneurship, Rice University
