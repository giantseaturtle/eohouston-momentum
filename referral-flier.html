Stop grinding.

Momentum is a two-year accelerator built by entrepreneurs, for entrepreneurs. It is for Houston business owners who have proven they can sell and are ready to build the company behind it, and scale past $1 million in revenue in under two years.

Built by operators, taught with Rice. The curriculum was written by Al Danto, an EO Houston founding member, exited founder, Momentum's Program Director, and Senior Lecturer in Entrepreneurship at Rice University, together with Jacky Fischer, owner of 3 Men Movers. Classes are taught by Rice faculty in partnership with Rice University's Lilie Lab for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, alongside EO Houston members who have already scaled past $1 million.

What you actually get

Not a seminar. An operating system for your business.

You work on your own company the whole way through. Eight months of in-person classes a year, a confidential peer Forum that meets monthly, and a community that shows up for each other.

01 · EDUCATION

Systems that scale

Eight months of classes on delegation, team, numbers, and leadership. Year 1 ends with a custom Operating Manual for running your business without you in every seat.

02 · FORUM

Your personal board

A confidential group of 6 to 8 non-competing owners, coached by trained EO Houston members, meeting monthly for four hours on your hardest business and personal calls.

03 · COMMUNITY

The EO network

Chapter learning events, socials, and the annual gala. Finish Momentum and step into EO Forum and a global network of 20,000+ entrepreneurs in 62 countries.

“Before the program I was working 13 to 16 hours a day, 7 days a week, and felt like an employee in the company I built. With coaching, structure, and support I stepped back, redefined my role, and began working on the business.” Rosalee Murugan, Owner, RLD Bookkeeping and Tax Solutions, Momentum member

Who qualifies

  • Owner or co-founder of a business
  • Yearly revenue over $250,000
  • Ready to build past $1M in under two years

Investment: Year 1 $3,500, Year 2 $3,000, plus a one-time $500 initiation fee. Year 1 is prorated to the month you join.
